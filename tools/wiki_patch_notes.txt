Update 1:
New
Three new characters: Gold Ship (Rushdown), Vodka (Vortex), and Daiwa Scarlet (Zoner).
Control mapping for keyboard and gamepad.
A Combos page in the in-game move list, and this wiki.
Gameplay
EX rework: motion + L and M are the free versions. Motion + H is the EX and costs 50 Spurt. The Skill button no longer does EX.
The guard button is gone. Block by holding back. Taunt moved to L+H.
Holding back cancels a dash instantly.
Walk and dash speeds are closer together, backdash invincibility is shorter (4 frames for most), double jump is gone, and air dash now needs height, can't attack for 8 frames, and has landing recovery.
Combos: a 9-frame input buffer that remembers your direction and motion, a bit more hitstun on normals, less pushback on hit, easier juggles, higher 5H launchers, and jump cancels only after a launcher.
Projectiles can now travel at angles, along the ground and from the air. Two can be out at once, and they can be reflected.
Characters
Special Week is now an All-Rounder. Guts! is replaced by Last Spurt Tackle, an armored dash.
Silence Suzuka is now Hit and Run: new Silent Flicker (kick then hop away), Turbine Roll moved to ←↙↓↘→, teleport and double jump removed, +10% damage, 8,500 health.

Update 1.5

Team Spica is complete. Three new characters bring the roster to eight.
New characters
Mejiro McQueen (Bait and Punish): long pokes, the Noble Parry counter, and a Punish passive that adds 20% damage on recovering opponents.
Tokai Teio (Glass Cannon): specials chain into each other on hit for the biggest combos in the game. Least health, no free reversal, and Miracle Mode granting a 15% damage bonus below 30% health.
Kitasan Black (Shoto): fireball, invincible uppercut, a whirlwind kick on the ground and in the air, and a step-in punch.
Systems
New move properties: counter stances, moves that pass through projectiles (several EX moves), special chains and comeback damage.
The move list shows each character's passive traits.


Update 1.6
New look
New UI for all menus and the HUD: starting-gate colors, tote-board timers, saddlecloth name plates and rosettes for round wins.
Added a course select, a VS screen before each match and a results board after it.
Rounds start with the starting gate opening, and knockouts end in a photo finish.
Stages
Three new stages: Tracen Training Track, Nakayama Nights, and Winning Live.
Music
Added music.
P1 picks the battle theme after the course. It defaults to P1's runner. Online, the host picks.
If a match goes to round 3, the Third Round Theme takes over.
Music and effects volume are in Options, and you can listen to every track there.
Combos
New damage scaling based on the starter, with a 13% drop per hit from the 3rd hit (minimum 25%, supers 50%).
Added ground bounce (j.H in an air combo) and wall bounce (some EX knockdowns), once each per combo.
Juggle points, hitstun decay after 2 seconds, and a combo limit of 32 hits or 7 seconds.
The combo counter shows the scaling for the next hit.
Training
New Combo Trials mode with 51 trials and demos.
Input display, frame meter, and dummy recording in Training.
CPU
Rebuilt the CPU. Four levels: Rookie, Normal, Hard, and G1.
Quality of life
Rematch from the results screen.
Random slot on character select.
Reduce flashes & shake and fullscreen options.
The online room panel works with a gamepad.
Menu sounds, plus a bell and gate sound at the start of each round.
Fixes
The CPU could super while airborne or right out of hitstun.
Supers at the end of a combo sometimes only did the first hit.
The menu music didn't start until you opened a menu.
Leaving a match during a KO could skip a round win in the next match.
Some of Teio's frames had a green square behind her.


Update 1.7

New
Uma Versus has a logo and a browser tab icon.
Daiwa Scarlet
Number One Finish is now a unique super: a volley of five golden roses and a big finishing rose instead of a rush. Each shot can hit, be blocked, or miss.
The shots go through normal projectiles, can't be reflected, and don't use juggle points.
Fixes
Fixed Gold Ship flying off the top of the screen when Golshi Dropkick!! was cancelled into a super in the air. Same for Suzuka's Silent Flicker.
Fixed matches getting stuck if a super cinematic was cut short, for example by time running out.
Fixed matches getting stuck if a projectile hit someone in the middle of a throw.
Fixed super cinematics near the edge pushing the opponent off the stage.
Fixed a crash when resetting Training during a super flash.
Super cinematics now use the character's damage multiplier and Miracle Mode, so Suzuka's and Teio's supers deal proper damage now.


Update 1.8

1.8.2
Fixed a freeze of about a twentieth of a second at the start of each match, when the battle theme loaded. Music now loads in the background after the game opens.
Hit sounds reuse their audio instead of making new audio on every hit, which cuts down on small stutters mid-fight.
Winning Live and Nakayama Nights draw their lights and snow with less work per frame.
Press F3 to show frames per second, frame time, and the worst frame in the last second.
Same build as 1.8.1, so 1.8.1 and 1.8.2 can play each other online.
1.8.1
The Sharp text option is now Graphics: Low / Medium / High in Options and the pause menu. Low is the original 960×540 picture, Medium is sharper, High is full resolution. Medium is the default.
Smoother frame pacing on 60 Hz screens, and less work per frame on 120 Hz and higher.
Fixed pressing A (Skill) to confirm a menu or unpause doing a skill as the match starts. A button that's still held from a menu is ignored until you let go.
Accounts
Added optional accounts. Sign up from Account on the main menu with an email, password, display name, and favourite runner. Guests can still play online.
Controls, volume, options, and trial progress save to your account and load when you sign in.
Online matches are saved to your match history. The Account screen shows your record, win rate, most-played runner and recent results.

Your email is only used to sign in. Display names, favourite runners and online results are public. Settings are private.
Display
The game now renders at your screen's resolution, so text and menus are sharper.
