# Tokai Teio

**Tokai Teio** is a glass cannon. Her specials chain into each other for huge damage, but she has the lowest health in the game.

## Overview

**Win fast or lose fast.** Turns one opening into huge damage, but barely has any defense. Rounds can end in two combos either way.

After the last hit of one skill, input another and it comes out right away. Each skill can be used once per chain, so a full chain is something like Teio Step › Imperial Rush › Emperor's Rise. Below 30% health, Miracle Mode gives her +15% damage for a comeback.

**Strengths**

- Highest damage in the cast (×1.12)
- Specials chain into each other on hit
- Miracle Mode comeback below 30%

**Weaknesses**

- Lowest health (8,000)
- No invincible reversal without meter

## Skills

#### Teio Step (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Dashing punch that chains into other specials. L short, M long.
- EX: Passes through projectiles and wall bounces.

#### Imperial Rush (↓↙← + L / M / H)

- Flurry of punches that ends in a launcher.
- L 3 hits, M 4 hits.
- EX: 7 hits and more damage.

#### Emperor's Rise (→↓↘ + L / M / H)

- Uppercut that beats jump-ins, but NOT invincible.
- EX: Invincible start.

#### Miracle Leap (←↙↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Leaping overhead kick. L short hop, M long.
- EX: Invincible start and knocks down.

## Tips

- Input the next special after the current one's last hit. The 9-frame buffer gives you a little room.
- Miracle Leap is an overhead. Mix it with 2L and 2M.
