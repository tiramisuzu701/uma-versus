# Vodka

**Vodka** is a vortex. She gets a hard knockdown, then makes you guess high, low, left, right or throw.

## Overview

**Knock down, make them guess.** Gets a knockdown that takes away the opponent's wake-up options, then runs a mixup. Guess wrong and you're back on the floor for another one.

Everything starts with a hard knockdown: Full Throttle, Pit Stop, EX Drift Hop or EX Drift Drop. No quick rise means they always get up on the same frame, so her setups always line up. L Drift Hop lands in front, M crosses over, meaty 2L is low, Drift Drop and j.M are overheads, and a walk-up Pit Stop beats blocking.

**Strengths**

- Consistent setups after hard knockdowns
- j.L and j.M cross up
- Command grab that only Counter Spurt escapes

**Weaknesses**

- Rev Kick isn't invincible on the ground
- Has to earn that first knockdown

## Skills

#### Full Throttle (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Rushing knee with a hard knockdown (no quick rise). L short, M long.
- EX: Invincible start and wall bounces.

#### Pit Stop (←↙↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Command grab (untechable) that leaves them right in front.
- EX: Longer range and more damage.

#### Drift Hop (↓↙← + L / M / H)

- Overhead hop kick.
- L lands in front, M crosses over.
- EX: Invincible start and knocks down.

#### Rev Kick (→↓↘ + L / M / H)

- Anti-air knee, NOT invincible on the ground.
- EX: Fully invincible start.

#### Drift Drop (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Fast low-angle overhead dive kick. L flatter, M steeper.
- EX: Hard knockdown.
