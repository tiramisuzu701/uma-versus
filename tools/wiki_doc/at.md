# Agnes Tachyon

**Agnes Tachyon** is a set-play character. She leaves potions on the floor that burst later, then makes you deal with them.

## Overview

**Set traps, then fight around them.** Strong once her setup is out, weak while she's building it and on defence.

Test Tube Toss lobs a potion that stays as a trap, Delayed Reaction sets a flask on a timer, and Catalyst sets every potion off at once. Up to three can be out. Potions arm a moment after they land and burst when stepped on, so walking forward is dangerous and a knockdown next to a potion is a big setup. Any attack or projectile that touches a potion pops it harmlessly.

**Strengths**

- Controls where the opponent can walk
- Catalyst sets off potions whenever she wants
- Strong setups after a knockdown

**Weaknesses**

- Low health (9,000)
- Slow to set up, and any hit pops a potion

## Skills

Every potion she leaves on the floor arms, then goes off when stepped on, on a timer, or with Catalyst. Any hit or projectile pops it. (This line is added to each potion skill on the wiki automatically.)

#### Test Tube Toss (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Lobs a potion that stays on the floor as a trap. L near, M far.
- EX: Two potions, one near and one far.

#### Delayed Reaction (↓↙← + L / M / H)

- Sets a flask that goes off on its own 1.5 seconds later. L at her feet, M further out.
- EX: Shorter fuse and bigger burst.

#### Catalyst (←↙↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Sets off every potion she has out, right now.
- EX: Invincible while she does it.

#### Overdose Spark (→↓↘ + L / M / H)

- Beats jump-ins, but NOT invincible.
- EX: Invincible start.

#### Flask Drop (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Drops a potion straight down onto the floor, once per jump.
- EX: Bursts on landing.

## Tips

- L Test Tube Toss lands close and can hit them in a combo. M lands farther away as a trap.
- Delayed Reaction on a knocked-down opponent goes off as they get up.
