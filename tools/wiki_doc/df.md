# Dantsu Flame

**Dantsu Flame** is a footsies character. She has fast feet and a long poke that turns into a combo, but she's weak on defence.

## Overview

**Win the space.** Walks in and out at the edge of her range, waiting for you to whiff, then punishes.

She has the fastest walk in the cast, a quick dash and backdash, and normals that recover fast. Flame Thrust is her signature: a long poke that's safe at the tip, and if it hits, press S again for Flame Twist, which launches into a combo. Cheese Wheel rolls in low over a short distance to control the ground in front of her.

**Strengths**

- Fastest walk speed (4.0)
- A poke that turns into a combo on hit
- Footwork steps in or out in an instant

**Weaknesses**

- Fromage Rising only beats air attacks without meter
- Needs to win neutral to get anything

## Skills

#### Flame Thrust (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Long poke, safe at the tip. L faster, M longer.
- On hit, press S (or L/M/H) again for Flame Twist, a launcher.
- EX: Passes through projectiles and wall bounces on its own.

#### Cheese Wheel (↓↙← + L / M / H)

- A slow cheese wheel rolls along the floor as a low. Short range.
- EX: Tosses a cheese wedge in an arc first, then the wheel.

#### Footwork (←↙↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- A quick step to bait and punish. L steps back, M steps in.
- EX: Steps right through them to the other side.

#### Fromage Rising (→↓↘ + L / M / H)

- Beats jump-ins, but NOT invincible.
- EX: Invincible start.

- EX Cheese Wheel covers a jump and the ground at once.
