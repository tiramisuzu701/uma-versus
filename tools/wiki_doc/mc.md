# Manhattan Cafe

**Manhattan Cafe** is a puppet character. Her skills send her Friend to attack from across the screen, but Cafe can't act until the Friend is done.

## Overview

**Fight through the Friend.** Her skills send the Friend, Sunday Silence, to attack for her. Lose the Friend and she loses most of her offence.

Phantom Rush charges across the screen, Shadow Sweep is a low, Night Drop falls from above as an overhead, and Rising Shade is an anti-air. Cafe can't act until the Friend's attack is over, so a blocked Friend skill leaves her open. Her strength is where the Friend can reach: full screen with Phantom Rush, right on top of you with Night Drop.

**Strengths**

- Attacks reach from anywhere on screen
- Low sweep and overhead drop from the Friend
- Friend skills combo off her normals

**Weaknesses**

- Can't act until the Friend finishes, so blocked skills are punishable
- Hit the Friend and Cafe loses her offence

## Skills

Skills named *Friend:* are carried out by Sunday Silence. Cafe can't act until the Friend's attack ends. If the Friend is hit while she attacks, she's gone for 4 seconds.

#### Friend: Phantom Rush (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- The Friend dashes forward and strikes. L short, M long.
- EX: Cafe is invincible, and the Friend hits twice and wall bounces.

#### Friend: Shadow Sweep (↓↙← + L / M / H)

- The Friend sweeps low in front of Cafe. L close, M far.
- EX: Two sweeps and a hard knockdown.

#### Friend: Night Drop (←↙↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- The Friend drops on them from above as an overhead. L in front of them, M right on top.
- EX: Faster and knocks down.

#### Friend: Rising Shade (→↓↘ + L / M / H)

- The Friend uppercuts beside Cafe. Cafe herself is NOT invincible.
- EX: Cafe is invincible too.

#### Friend: Night Dive (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- The Friend dives down from beside Cafe as an overhead. L flatter, M steeper.
- EX: Knocks down.

## Tips

- Night Drop takes a moment to fall, so time it with a low from Cafe.
- Cafe can't act until the Friend finishes, so a blocked Friend skill is punishable. Use them in combos or from range.
