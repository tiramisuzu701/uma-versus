# Mejiro McQueen

**Mejiro McQueen** is a bait and punish character. She has long pokes, a counter stance, and extra damage on anyone caught recovering.

## Overview

**Make them commit.** Waits for you to do something, then makes you pay for it. Struggles against someone patient.

5M and Stayer's Lance outrange almost everything, so walking at her is risky, and swinging at her up close might run into Noble Parry. Her passive, Punish, gives +20% damage and extra hitstun on the first hit against someone stuck in recovery or a long landing.

**Strengths**

- Stayer's Lance is the longest-reaching poke in the game
- Counter stance that catches high and low strikes
- Most health in the cast (10,500) and PUNISH! damage

**Weaknesses**

- Not much offense of her own
- Noble Parry loses to throws, and to projectiles unless it's EX

## Skills

#### Stayer's Lance (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Long kick for punishing from range. L faster, M longer.
- EX: Passes through projectiles and wall bounces.

#### Noble Parry (↓↙← + L / M / H)

- Counter stance that catches a strike and answers with Noble Riposte (invincible, 2 hits).
- Catches strikes on frames 3–24. Throws and projectiles beat it.
- EX: Longer window, also parries projectiles, bigger riposte.

#### Mejiro Ascension (→↓↘ + L / M / H)

- Invincible anti-air.

#### Long-Distance Stride (←↙↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Very long low slide (unsafe on block).
- EX: Passes through projectiles and hard knockdown.

## Tips

- Noble Parry catches strikes from frame 3 but not throws. Use it when you expect an attack, not on reflex.
- Block something unsafe and punish it. The PUNISH! bonus goes on the first hit of your combo.
