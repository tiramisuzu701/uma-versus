# Kitasan Black

**Kitasan Black** is a shoto. She has a fireball, an invincible uppercut, a spin kick that also works in the air, and a step-in punch.

## Overview

**The classic kit.** Easy to pick up, solid everywhere, a little predictable.

Festival Drum is a fireball with quick recovery, Matsuri Uppercut is the best invincible reversal in the cast, Festival Whirlwind goes over lows, and Kitasan Straight is a step-in punch that's barely minus. Fireball until they jump, uppercut the jump, then use Straight and Whirlwind to take your turn up close.

**Strengths**

- Fireball recovers fast (28 frames total)
- Strongest invincible uppercut (1,300 damage)
- Whirlwind works on the ground and in the air

**Weaknesses**

- Predictable once people learn her
- Matsuri Uppercut is very punishable on block

## Skills

#### Festival Drum (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Fireball with quick recovery. L slow, M fast.
- EX: Fast 2-hit fireball that knocks down.

#### Matsuri Uppercut (→↓↘ + L / M / H)

- Invincible uppercut, the strongest in the cast.

#### Festival Whirlwind (↓↙← + L / M / H)

- Advancing spin kick that passes over lows.
- L 2 spins, M 3 spins.
- EX: 5 spins and carries farther.

#### Sky Whirlwind (↓↙← + L / M / H)

- Air spin kick, once per jump.
- L 1 spin, M 2 spins.
- EX: 3 spins and knocks down.

#### Kitasan Straight (←↙↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Step-in punch, nearly safe on block. L short, M long.
- EX: Passes through projectiles and wall bounces.
