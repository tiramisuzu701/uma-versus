# Silence Suzuka

**Silence Suzuka**** ****i****s** a hit and run character. She aims to get in and out quick while dealing lethal damage quickly.

## Overview

**Hit and Run****.** Big damage and great escape tools, but low health and not much to do once she's corner locked.

M Great Escape runs through the opponent and keeps going, and Silent Flicker kicks and then hops way back, so a lot of her moves are minus on paper but safe because she's out of range.

She hits harder than anyone except Teio (×1.1), with a quick dash, a long backdash and the only air dash in the game.

**Strengths**

- ×1.1 damage (second only to Teio)
- Moves that leave her out of punish range
- Air dash and the longest backdash

**Weaknesses**

- Below-average health (9,000)
- Lead the Pack has short invincibility
- Not many defensive corner options

## Skills

#### Great Escape (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Sprint strike.
- L stops in front, M runs through and away.
- EX: Invincible, runs through and launches.

#### Silent Flicker (↓↙← + L / M / H)

## Tips

- L Great Escape stops in front and M runs through. Mix them up so they can't punish on reaction.
- Blocked M Silent Flicker leaves you about 240 px away (200 for L, 270 for EX). Very few moves reach that.
