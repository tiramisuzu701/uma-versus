# Daiwa Scarlet

**Daiwa Scarlet** is a zoner. She shoots straight, up, low and from the air, and has a reflector, but she's weak up close.

## Overview

**Control the screen.** Keeps you out with projectiles at different angles and speeds. Low damage, so she wins by chipping away and never letting you in.

Scarlet Shot goes straight (two can be out), High Top Line angles up to stop jumps, Ground Rose runs along the floor and Sky Shot fires down from the air. Rose Barrier reflects projectiles and knocks away anyone too close, back to shooting range.

**Strengths**

- Projectiles at four different angles
- Reflector that also pushes people out
- Long normals and a good backdash

**Weaknesses**

- Lowest damage (×0.95) and slowest dash
- In trouble once someone gets in

## Skills

#### Scarlet Shot (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Projectile, two can be on screen. L slow, M fast.
- EX: Two fast shots.

#### High Top Line (↓↙← + L / M / H)

- Anti-air shot angled upward. L flatter, M steeper.
- EX: Fires both angles.

#### Ground Rose (←↙↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Low projectile along the ground. L short range, M full screen.
- EX: Faster and hits twice.

#### Rose Barrier (→↓↘ + L / M / H)

- Reflects projectiles and pushes them away.
- EX: Invincible, longer reflect window and wall bounces.

#### Sky Shot (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Air shot angled downward, once per jump. L flatter, M steeper.
- EX: Two shots.

## Super

#### Number One Finish (S+H)

- A volley of 5 golden roses and one big finishing rose. Each shot can hit, be blocked or miss on its own.
- Up close to midrange all six hit and end in a hard knockdown.
- Goes through normal projectiles, can't be reflected and costs no juggle points, so it works as a combo ender.
- Punishable on block up close, safe from range.

## Tips

- Mix Ground Rose (low) with Scarlet Shot (mid) so they can't just block one way.
- L High Top Line covers a forward jump. M is for jumps from closer in.
