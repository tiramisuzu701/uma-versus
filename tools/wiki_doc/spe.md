# Special Week

**Special Week** is an all-rounder. She has a fireball, an uppercut, an advancing spin kick and an armored tackle.

## Overview

**A bit of everything.** Has something for every range: a projectile, an anti-air, a way in and a way out. Few matchups are bad for her, and few are good. Good for beginners.

Special Week has tools at all ranges. Dream Shot covers midscreen, Rising Dream stops jump-ins, Spurt Tornado travels in over low pokes and Last Spurt Tackle armors through a hit or a fireball to gap close.

**Strengths**

- A tool for every situation
- EX Rising Dream is fully invincible
- Simple combos that end in knockdown

**Weaknesse****s**

- Rising Dream and Tackle are very punishable on block

## Skills

#### Dream Shot (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- L slow, M fast.
- EX: Fast 2-hit fireball that knocks down.

#### Rising Dream (→↓↘ + L / M / H)

- Invincible anti-air.

#### Spurt Tornado (↓↙← + L / M / H)

- Advancing spin kick that passes over lows.
- L 2 hits, M 3 hits.
- EX: 5 hits and carries farther.

#### Last Spurt Tackle (←↙↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Armored charge, absorbs 1 hit or projectile.
- EX: 2-hit armor, faster and farther, and wall bounces.

## Tips

- L Dream Shot is slow enough to walk or dash in behind.
