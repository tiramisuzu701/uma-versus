# Gold Ship

**Gold Ship** is a rushdown. She has the fastest normals in the game, a command dash with mixups, and Golshi Dropkick!!.

## Overview

**Stay in their face.** Fast attacks that are plus on block, and ways to keep going after a blocked string. Wants to start pressure and never stop. Struggles at long range.

5L is 4 frames and plus on block, 2L chains into itself up to twice, and Barrage is plus too. Golshi Rush is where the mixups come from: slide (low), hop chop (overhead), grab (can't be teched) or stop. Dash cancels (→→ after a normal, 50 Spurt) keep the pressure going, though she can't attack for the first 10 frames of the dash.

**Strengths**

- Fastest normals in the game (4f 5L, 6f 5M)
- Lots of plus-on-block pressure
- Four-way command dash mixup

**Weaknesses**

- No invincible reversal (EX Golshi Rush is invincible, but it's a dash)
- Golshi Dropkick!! is around −22 on block

## Skills

#### Golshi Rush (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Command dash.
- During the dash: L low slide, M overhead, H grab, S stop (50 Spurt, like a dash cancel).
- EX: Invincible and dashes farther.

#### Golshi Dropkick!! (→↓↘ + L / M / H)

- Huge damage and beats jump-ins, but NOT invincible.
- EX: 2-hit armor and wall bounces.

#### Barrage (↓↙← + L / M / H)

- Rapid jabs, plus on block.
- L 3 jabs (+2), M 4 jabs (+0).
- EX: 8 jabs, very plus on block.

#### Golshi Mode (←↙↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- 6-second buff: faster, +1 blockstun and +8% damage (10-second cooldown).
- EX: Instant and lasts 10 seconds.

#### Sky Dropkick (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Diving overhead dropkick. L shallow, M steep.
- EX: Knocks down.

## Tips

- End blocked strings with L Barrage (+2) to keep your turn.
- Out of the dash: grab beats blocking, slide beats standing guard, chop beats crouching guard.
