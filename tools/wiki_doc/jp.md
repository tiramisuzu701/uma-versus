# Jungle Pocket

**Jungle Pocket** is a grappler. She has three command grabs, an armored charge and 11,000 health, but she's slow to get in.

## Overview

**Get close, then grab.** Command grabs that can't be blocked or teched and do huge damage. Lots of health and armor to get in, but slow.

Up close you have to guess: block and Pocket Buster grabs you, press a button or jump and her normals and Lion Charge are waiting. Getting in is the hard part. Lion Charge armors through a poke, Jungle Rush grabs from half a screen away, and Wild Catch grabs people out of the air. Her super is a grab that can catch someone in hitstun, so it doubles as a combo ender.

**Strengths**

- Command grabs that can't be blocked or teched
- Most health (11,000)
- Anti-air grab and an armored charge to get in

**Weaknesses**

- Slowest walk speed
- Command grabs whiff on anyone jumping, backdashing or in hitstun, and are very punishable when they do

## Skills

#### Pocket Buster (←↙↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Command grab with huge damage. Can't be blocked or teched.
- L has less reach but recovers faster.
- EX: Invincible start, longer reach and more damage.

#### Jungle Rush (↓↘→ + L / M / H)

- Running grab that covers half the screen. L short, M long.
- EX: Runs through projectiles, faster and more damage.

#### Wild Catch (→↓↘ + L / M / H)

- Anti-air grab. Catches jumpers, misses anyone on the ground.
- EX: Fully invincible start and more damage.

#### Lion Charge (↓↙← + L / M / H)

- Armored shoulder charge that absorbs 1 hit.
- EX: 2-hit armor and wall bounces.

## Super

#### Wild Typhoon (S+H)

- A command grab: it can't be blocked, and if it catches them the whole cinematic plays out.
- Catches someone in hitstun, so cancel into it at the end of a grounded combo. Still misses a juggled opponent.

## Tips

- Grabs beat blocking. Once they start jumping to escape, use Wild Catch or 2H instead.
- Lion Charge absorbs one hit (two with EX). Use it through pokes and fireballs.
